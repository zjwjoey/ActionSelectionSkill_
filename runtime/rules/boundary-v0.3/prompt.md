# BOUNDARY-V03-1

Use only original product title to understand the sold item. Category helps find
source records, not proof of type or suitability. No images, price or details.
Do not infer plastic, lithium chemistry, water tanks, bare module, portable or
stick form if not explicitly stated. Omit unsupported attributes from both the
Chinese type and reasons. Separate ordinary function hypotheses from facts.

Provide a literal subject span, concrete Chinese type, bounded function/need,
sold form, dependency, candidate boundary and source uncertainties. Forms:
ordinary_good, bundle, universal_accessory, host_component, installed_component,
whole_machine, whole_system, unknown. Dependency corresponds to the form;
universal daily accessories are distinct from host-specific parts. Ordinary
paper-roll holders and general hanging hardware are not industrial systems.

CORE_GOOD within-scope needs a real Action reference of same_type, related_type
or specific_need, with subject coverage. category_only, host_only, none or
partial_bundle cannot establish whole-item retention. The reference must be
from the frozen eligible Action index, never invented. Its original title also
limits what can be claimed. Missing reference alone never excludes.

FURNITURE_REVIEW, LARGE_FORM_REVIEW, SMALL_APPLIANCE_REVIEW, POWER_TOOL_REVIEW,
CHEMICAL_MATERIAL_REVIEW, POWER_GARDEN_REVIEW, INSTALLATION_REVIEW, COMPONENT,
UNCONFIRMED, COMPLEX_AUTOMATION and FIXED_GATE_SYSTEM stay review. Robot feature
count is evidence about structure, not an approved business exclusion policy.
Ordinary daily vacuums with valid concrete Action references may be CORE_GOOD;
do not confuse them with robot cleaning systems. Generic missing performance
facts are not by themselves reasons to exclude normal daily goods.

Outside proposals only for clearly sold whole MAJOR_APPLIANCE,
FIXED_WATER_HEATING or INDUSTRIAL_COMPLETE_MACHINE. Quote the literal boundary
type phrase as boundary_evidence_span. Parts/consumables never inherit a host's
boundary. Uncertain supply/form stays unknown/review. Do not force exclusion
quotas. Every decision is a candidate, never human Gold or published policy.

Keep old evidence and use exact source/prompt/method/model cache bindings. For
old60 migrated reviews explicitly preserve parent provenance rather than
claiming a new independent test. Freeze before new300; do not alter this prompt
or rules in response to the validation records. Record validation findings for
the next version instead.
