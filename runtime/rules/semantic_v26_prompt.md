# V26-P1 — title-only retail semantics

Input: exact source ID/hash, Spanish original product title, category_l1, eligible Action title evidence and candidate group cards. Do not read price, images, details or sealed Action holdout.

First identify the sold object (literal title span) separately from its purpose or named host. Interpret its specific type, ordinary function and consumer need; label these interpretations as AI hypotheses. State physical form only when the title supports it. Classify dependency as standalone, universal_accessory, host_specific, installed or unknown. Preserve uncertainty: no guessed material, dimensions, performance, demand or purchasing motive.

Retrieve original-title and subject candidates, then compare the actual sold objects. A: same detailed type; B: related concrete type; C: different objects serving a stated specific consumer need; D: broad category only; E: no supported relation. Cite an existing eligible Action SKU and literal original-name evidence for A-D. A real SKU proves existence, not relevance. Group membership and inherited historical labels remain candidates and cannot alone prove A-C.

Retain may use A/B/C with specific explanation and compatible independent form. D/E alone stay observation. Missing reference is not exclusion. Furniture and dependent dedicated parts default to research observation under the Owner's daily-first scope. Unknown installation/performance is uncertainty, not a negative fact. Professional/industrial adjectives do not by themselves exclude ordinary tools.

Experimental exclusion requires an unambiguous sold subject and explicit boundary evidence (whole major appliance, split air conditioner, fixed water heater, industrial control or clearly specialized installation equipment), plus a counterexample check. Never inherit host boundaries onto bags, covers, magnets or cleaning accessories. No quota for exclusions. Every boundary is an AI candidate, not human-validated policy.

Return a source-bound structured proposal with literal subject, type, function/need/relation explanation, dependency/form, group, A-E relation, real Action SKU/title span, uncertainty, boundary evidence and one of 保留/观察/用不到. Leave all human labels blank. Do not revise frozen rules after independent sample exposure. No AI self-assessment accuracy claims.
