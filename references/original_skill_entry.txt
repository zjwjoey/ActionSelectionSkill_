## Decisions and evidence

Distinguish same detailed type, related type, shared consumer need, broad task/category only, and no clear relationship. A broad noun or brand match cannot establish a specific type. Preserve the main object, named purpose qualifiers, multiple components and dependencies; a noun late in a purpose clause is not necessarily the item sold.

Historical V2 Amazon results use four experimental states: `HIGH_MATCH`, `POTENTIAL_MATCH`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `LOW_MATCH`. Do not rewrite them. New boundary experiments normalize output to `保留`, `观察`, `用不到`. No found reference never causes exclusion by itself. A professional label or unknown optional/safety attributes alone cannot justify exclusion. Retain every original row.

Every reference must bind to a real Action SKU, original title and source row. Leave absent references blank. Ordinary-use and consumer-need labels are hypotheses; titles do not establish quality, effects, demand, sales, procurement motives or independent usability. Do not label output Gold or production validated without independent evidence.
